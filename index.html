1 · What is WatchPick?

WatchPick is a personal cinema discovery app for people who care about what they watch. It shows films streaming in India right now, filtered by your taste, with verified availability across 8 platforms.

Built for world cinema, philosophy films, critically acclaimed titles, music documentaries, brilliant screenplays, and visually extraordinary cinema — all verified streaming in India via JustWatch data.

Access: shomikprth.pythonanywhere.com/watch/watchpick.html →

2 · Getting Started

Open in Chrome
Go to the WatchPick URL → in Chrome on your phone.
2

Letterboxd →
Opens Letterboxd → search for that exact film. Letterboxd is a dedicated film community of nearly 30 million users who rate on a 5-star scale (with half-stars). Its ratings run 0.3–0.5 stars colder than TMDB because the audience takes film seriously — making it the most trustworthy rating source for world cinema, philosophy films, and arthouse titles. A film rated 4.0 on Letterboxd by 200,000 cinephiles is a stronger signal than an 8.2 on TMDB from a general audience. Tap to read reviews and see what dedicated film watchers think before you commit your evening.

10 · FAQ
My list disappeared

You likely cleared browser cache or opened a downloaded file. Open the hosted URL → My List → enter passphrase → Load ↓.

A film I know is on Netflix is not showing

Availability is from JustWatch → via TMDB. Rights change frequently — data may lag by a day or two for very recent additions.

Trailers are not playing

Trailers only work on the hosted URL — not a downloaded file. If "Video unavailable", the studio has disabled embedding; find it directly on YouTube →.

Feed keeps showing same titles

Tap Load Page N ↓ for new pages. If many titles are marked Seen or Not Interested they are filtered out — use ↑ Refresh for a completely fresh batch.

YouTube shows Rent/Buy under Stream only

JustWatch India does not consistently report YouTube Premium streaming as "flatrate". This is a JustWatch data limitation, not a WatchPick bug. Some Stream titles do appear.

10 · FAQ
My list disappeared.
You likely opened a downloaded file or cleared browser cache. Open the hosted URL, go to My List, enter your passphrase, and tap Load ↓.
A film I know is on Netflix is not showing.
India availability is sourced from JustWatch via TMDB and is updated regularly but not in real time. Rights change frequently — recently added titles may have a short lag.
Trailers are not playing.
Trailers only play from the hosted URL (not a downloaded file). Some studios disable embedding — if a trailer shows "Video unavailable", it will still open on YouTube directly.
The feed keeps showing the same titles.
Tap Load Page N ↓ for genuinely new titles. If you have marked many titles as Seen they are filtered out — use ↻ Refresh for a completely fresh batch.
YouTube shows Rent/Buy even on Stream only.
JustWatch India does not consistently report YouTube Premium's streaming catalogue as flatrate. Most YouTube content in India appears as rental/purchase data — this is a JustWatch data limitation, not a WatchPick bug.

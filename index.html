1 · What is WatchPick?

WatchPick is a personal cinema discovery app for people who care about what they watch. It shows films streaming in India right now, filtered by your taste, with verified availability across 8 platforms.

Built for world cinema, philosophy films, critically acclaimed titles, music documentaries, brilliant screenplays, and visually extraordinary cinema — with everything double-checked against real, up-to-date streaming information for India.

Access: watchpick-dun.vercel.app →

2 · Getting Started
Install on your phone
1
Open in Chrome
Go to the WatchPick URL → in Chrome on your phone.
2
Add to Home Screen
Chrome menu (⋮) → Add to Home Screen → Add. WatchPick appears on your home screen and opens full-screen.
3
Set up Cloud Sync
My List → Cloud Sync panel → enter a passphrase → tap Save ↑. Use same passphrase on any device to Load ↓ your list.
Always open WatchPick from your home screen icon or saved bookmark — never from a copy of the page you've downloaded to your phone.
3 · The Three Tabs
Discover
Search
Search bar — title, director, or actor. Gold person card appears for people — tap for their India filmography. Tap the mic icon to search by speaking instead of typing.
Films / Both / Series
Toggle above the search bar — narrow Discover to movies only, TV series only, or both together. In Both, every card in Your Curation carries a small "Film" or "Series" tag next to its year so a mixed feed stays easy to scan — the tag drops away in Films-only or Series-only mode since it'd just repeat what the toggle already tells you.
Masters
Masters of Cinema — curated row of directors, writers, and cinematographers grouped by taste: Contemplative, World Cinema, Indian Cinema, Visual Craft, Screenplay, Cinematography. Open by default with the theme tags showing — tap the "Masters of Cinema" header to collapse or reopen the whole section. Tap a theme to reveal its portraits ("Click on any Theme to reveal Masters"), then tap any thumbnail to open that person's full India-available filmography ("Click on any thumbnail to open Filmography"). Once portraits are showing, tap "Collapse" to hide them again without closing the whole section.
New
New on Streaming — films added to Indian streaming in the last 60 days. Collapsed by default, right below Masters of Cinema — tap the header to open it, which loads the row and reveals its own Refresh button. If you want recency inside your main feed instead, use the Newest or Newest + Rated sort in Your Curation below.
Liked
Because You Liked — built from crew and cast overlap with your own Liked and Want to Watch titles, not an opaque algorithm: every card names exactly who connects it back ("because you liked X — also directed by Y" or "also stars Y"), so you can judge the recommendation for yourself instead of just trusting it. Appears once you've Liked or Want-to-Watch'd at least one title; collapsed by default, right below New on Streaming — tap the header to open it.
Soon
Upcoming From Who You Liked — same idea as Because You Liked, but looking forward: directors, writers, and actors from your Liked/Want to Watch titles who have something with a real, dated release still to come. Each card shows the release month and names exactly who connects it back, same as Because You Liked. Only counts titles with an actual announced date — "in production" with no date yet isn't shown. Appears once you've Liked or Want-to-Watch'd something; collapsed by default, right below Because You Liked.
Refresh
Fresh batch for your current filters.
Load Page ↓
Next page of results — genuinely new titles.
Every card has its own quick-action row — bookmark (Want), star (Liked), check (Seen), eye-slash (Not Interested), right from Discover with no need to open the detail page. Hover any icon on desktop, or long-press it on a touchscreen, to see what it means.
My List
Shows: Want to Watch
Want to Watch is always front and center here, with its own Sort (Recently Added, Top Rated, Newest, A–Z) — it never collapses. Liked and Seen each get a small chip beside the header, showing a count only when that bucket has titles; tap one to expand its grid right above the Want to Watch list, and tap "Want to Watch" itself to collapse both back down in one tap. Not Interested isn't listed anywhere — it just affects what Discover shows you (see Tag Reference).
Sync
Save ↑ backs your list up online. Load ↓ brings it down on any device.
Export
Downloads a backup file of your list — save it to Google Drive or anywhere safe.
Import
Restores your list from that backup file.

4 · The Filter Bar

Tap Filters next to the search box to open Genres, Language, Sort and Availability — instant filters without going into Settings.

Film Genres
All / None + a button for every genre you've turned on in Settings. Tap one to switch it on or off — gold means active. Shown whenever you're browsing Films or Both.
Series Genres
Its own All / None + a button per TV genre. Only appears when you're browsing Series or Both — these genres aren't in Settings, this is the only place to choose them. Genres that mean the same thing on both sides (Action, Comedy, Crime, Sci-Fi, and a few others) stay in sync automatically when you tap a Film Genre, so Series doesn't keep showing everything while Films is narrowed down. Genres unique to Film (Philosophy, World Cinema, Superhero and the like) don't have a Series match — adjust Series Genres here separately for those.
Lang
Filter the current feed by original language instantly — and pick more than one at once, even across groups (Tamil from India + Korean from International together, for example). Grouped by country instead of one long list — tap the "India" or "International" chevron to expand it for Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Bengali, Kannada, Marathi, Punjabi and Gujarati, or English, French, Korean, Japanese, Spanish, Italian, German, Persian, Turkish, Portuguese, Swedish, Polish, Danish, Mandarin and Arabic. Tapping a language toggles it on/off and keeps its group open so your picks stay visible. Tapping the group name itself (not the chevron) selects every language in that group at once — tap it again to clear the whole group. Selecting every language in BOTH groups at the same time is treated as the same thing as "All Languages" and snaps back to it automatically, since a real "everything" is faster and more complete than picking every language you see individually. "All Languages" itself clears every specific pick everywhere.
Sort
Defaults to Top Rated. Options: Top Rated, Mix, Newest, Oldest, Newest + Rated, A–Z. Mix pulls from the same well-rated pool as Top Rated but shuffles instead of ranking — useful for variety once you've already narrowed with filters, rather than seeing the identical ranked list every time. Changing sort restarts from Page 1.
Avail
All, Stream (subscription) only, or Rent/Buy only.
Reset
Top-right of the sheet — clears every filter back to its default in one tap.
Show Results
Bottom button — closes the sheet and returns to your updated Discover feed. Tapping outside the sheet does the same.
Newest + Rated: fetches by year descending, then sorts by rating within each year — best for recent acclaimed films.
5 · Title Detail Page

Tap any card to open the full detail view.

Info row
✓ Available in India · Year · ★ Rating · Number of votes · Runtime · Language · Age rating · Genres.
Actions
Want to Watch · ★ Liked · ✓ Seen · Not Interested. Tap the same one again to undo. Liked, Seen, and Not Interested all remove the title from Discover (see Tag Reference for how to bring each one back).
Where to Watch
Every India platform with Stream or Rent/Buy tags. The small text under each button (e.g. "Available in India · Opens Netflix Search") sets expectations before you tap — it opens that platform's search, not the title directly, since platforms don't support deep-linking to a specific title from outside their own app.
IMDb →
Opens the film's official IMDb page directly, when one is available. Useful for reviews from a wider audience, parental guidance, and fuller cast/crew credits.
Letterboxd →
Opens Letterboxd → search for that exact film. Letterboxd is a dedicated film community of nearly 30 million users who rate on a 5-star scale (with half-stars). Its ratings run 0.3–0.5 stars colder than the rating you see in WatchPick, because the audience takes film seriously — making it the most trustworthy rating source for world cinema, philosophy films, and arthouse titles. A film rated 4.0 on Letterboxd by 200,000 film lovers is a stronger signal than an 8.2 in WatchPick's own rating from a general audience. Tap to read reviews and see what dedicated film watchers think before you commit your evening.
▶ Trailers
Tap the thumbnail to play it right here in the app. Only works on the real WatchPick address.
Crew →
Tap Director, Writer, or Cinematographer name to open their India filmography.
Did You Know
Trivia section — appears when there's something to show. Surfaces special-thanks credits, notable crew roles (composer, executive producer, story credit), and recurring director–cinematographer collaborations, each linking to IMDb's trivia page for more.
Cast →
Tap any actor for their India-available filmography with platform badges.
More Like This
Similar films or shows, filtered to what's available in India — tap to explore.
← Back
Phone back gesture or ← returns to previous screen. ✕ Close exits the detail overlay entirely.

7 · Search

Type any title, director, actor, or crew member in the search bar on Discover.

Film results
Cards with India availability, platform badges, Stream/Rent tags.
Gold card
Shown when your search matches a person, not a title — tap to open their India filmography.
Filmography
"Streaming in India" (full brightness) and "Other notable work" (dimmed), each with its own Sort: Streaming First, Top Rated, Newest. Back button returns to search.

8 · Cloud Sync

Your list is saved right on your phone, and backed up online too. The online copy survives everything — even clearing your phone's data or switching devices.

1
Choose a passphrase
Memorable — e.g. your-name-films-2026. No email or sign-up needed.
2
Save ↑ regularly
Tap Save ↑ after any marking session. Backs up your full list online.
3
Load ↓ on new device
My List → enter passphrase → Load ↓. Full list restored instantly.
Passphrase is case-sensitive (capital and lowercase letters count as different). No recovery if forgotten. You can also use Export in My List to save a backup file to Google Drive.

Letterboxd →
For every film in WatchPick — especially world cinema, philosophy, and critically acclaimed titles — tap "View on Letterboxd" on the detail page. Letterboxd uses a 5-star scale rated by nearly 30 million dedicated film watchers. Its scores run colder than the rating you see in WatchPick (a 3.8 on Letterboxd is genuinely good; a 4.2 is exceptional). For the genres WatchPick covers, Letterboxd is the most honest measure of whether a film is worth your time.

10 · FAQ
My list disappeared

You likely cleared your phone's storage for the browser, or opened a saved copy of the page instead of the real one. Open the real WatchPick address → My List → enter passphrase → Load ↓.

A film I know is on Netflix is not showing

We check streaming availability using a trusted tracking service →. Streaming rights change often, so very recently added titles can take a day or two to show up correctly.

Trailers are not playing

Trailers only work on the real WatchPick address — not a downloaded copy of the page. If it says "Video unavailable", the studio has blocked it from playing here; find it directly on YouTube →.

Feed keeps showing same titles

Tap Load Page N ↓ for new pages. If many titles are marked Liked, Seen, or Not Interested they are filtered out — use Refresh for a completely fresh batch.

YouTube shows Rent/Buy when I think it should be Stream

Our streaming-info source doesn't always correctly flag YouTube Premium titles as included with your subscription, so they can show as Rent/Buy even when they're actually free to stream on Premium. That's a limitation of that data source, not a WatchPick bug — some titles do show correctly as Stream.
